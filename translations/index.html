About the translations

Dayleaf only uses translations that may be freely shared: historic editions whose copyright has expired, or texts whose owners allow it. None of the verses were translated by us. Every text comes from the published edition named below. The original Arabic of the Quran and Hebrew of the Torah are always shown in full.

English

  • Quran: Marmaduke Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930). Complete.
  • Bible: King James Version. Complete.
  • Torah: Jewish Publication Society, The Holy Scriptures (1917). Complete.

German

  • Quran: Lazarus Goldschmidt, Der Koran (1916). For some verses, the digital edition we use has no German text yet; these appear in English.
  • Bible: Lutherbibel 1912. Where the German verse numbering differs, a few verses appear in English.
  • Torah: Samson Raphael Hirsch, Der Pentateuch (1867–1878), for Genesis and Exodus. The other three books appear in English for now.

Turkish

Most Turkish translations of the Quran, the Bible and the Torah are still protected by copyright. For the older ones that are free to use, we could not yet find a reliable digital copy in their original wording. Until we can add a Turkish translation we are allowed to share, verses appear in English, always together with the original Arabic or Hebrew text. We are working on it.
