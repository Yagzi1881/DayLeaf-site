Support

Questions, ideas or a problem with the app? Write to us, we usually answer within a few days.

support@dayleaf.info

How do I change my topics?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and set how often each topic should appear.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

In Settings, under Daily reminder. You can also turn notifications off in your iPhone's settings.

How do I show or hide the holy books?

In Settings, under Holy scriptures. Each book can be switched on or off.

Why are some verses in English?

We only use translations that may be freely shared. Where no such translation exists yet in your language, the English one is shown.

Does Quoteleaf collect my data?

No. Everything you choose stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
