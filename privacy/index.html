Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Summary

Quoteleaf does not collect personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything you choose or save in the app (your topics, holy books, favourites, reminder time and reading position) stays on your device. We, the developer, never receive it.

The rest of this policy explains this in detail, describes the few situations where data is handled by others (Apple, Google, the apps you share to) or by us (only if you write to us), and sets out your rights under Swiss, EU and Turkish data protection law.

2. Who is responsible

The app is developed and published by:

Ali Yağız Coşkun, Switzerland
Email: support@dayleaf.info

In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer named above. Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/nDSG), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the developer is the "controller" (KVKK: "veri sorumlusu") for the limited personal data described in sections 10 and 11. For all other data described in this policy, we are not a controller, because we never receive or have access to that data.

3. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Quoteleaf app for iPhone (and, if released, for Android), in every version distributed by us, to our website dayleaf.info, and to messages you send us by email.

It does not cover the App Store, Google Play, your device's operating system or the apps you share content to. These are run by other companies under their own privacy policies (see sections 8 and 9).

4. Data stored only on your device

To work, the app saves the following information in its private storage on your device (on iPhone in the app's "UserDefaults" area, on Android in its "SharedPreferences" area):

  • whether you have completed the welcome screens;
  • your app language and appearance (light, dark or automatic);
  • the topics you chose and how often each should appear;
  • the holy books you chose to show (Quran, Bible, Torah), if any;
  • the quotes and verses you marked as favourites;
  • a history of which quote was shown on which day, so that quotes do not repeat too soon and you can look back at past days;
  • whether the daily reminder is on and at what time;
  • the page you last opened in each holy book reader.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app. Only you, on your device, can see or change it. It stays there until you change it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app deletes all of it, except copies in device backups (section 7).

Your choice of holy books, and possibly of some topics, could suggest your religious or philosophical beliefs. Such information is "sensitive personal data" under the FADP, a "special category of data" under Article 9 GDPR and "special quality personal data" under Article 6 KVKK. We therefore designed the app so that this choice never leaves your device and is never seen by us.

5. No internet connection, no third-party tools

All quotes, holy texts, translations and fonts are built into the app. The app does not connect to the internet, and we do not run any server for it. It contains no software from third parties for:

  • advertising;
  • analytics or usage statistics;
  • crash reporting;
  • social media log-in or tracking;
  • payment.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or any device identifier for advertising (such as Apple's IDFA). It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not create a profile of you.

The quote of the day is chosen by a calculation on your device, based only on the date and the topics you chose. This does not involve any automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6. Daily reminder notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your device for permission to show notifications. The reminders are "local notifications": the app schedules them on your device, and they are not sent through any server, ours or anyone else's. To show the reminder at the right time, the app reads your device's time zone setting. This also happens only on the device.

You can turn reminders off at any time in the app (Settings) or in your device's settings.

7. Device backups

If you have turned on iCloud Backup, a computer backup or Google's device backup, your operating system may include the app's stored settings (section 4) in that backup. This lets your settings and favourites return when you restore or move to a new device. Apple or Google handles these backups under their own privacy policies and your account settings. We have no access to them.

8. Sharing quotes and verses

When you tap "Share", the app creates the image or text on your device and hands it to your device's share menu. A temporary image file may be created in the app's cache for this purpose; the operating system deletes it later.

If you then choose another app (for example a messenger, email or social network), that app receives the content you chose to share and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive anything you share and cannot see what or with whom you share.

9. Apple and Google

Downloading the app. To download Quoteleaf, you use Apple's App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google processes your account, download and device information as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, email address or payment details from them.

Statistics and crash reports. Apple and Google give developers statistics about their apps. On iPhone this includes data such as the number of downloads, and crash reports only if you agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > "Share with App Developers"). Google offers comparable information through Google Play. This information is anonymous or aggregated: we cannot identify you from it, and we use it only to find and fix errors and to understand how many people use the app. You can switch this sharing off in your device settings at any time.

Beta testing (TestFlight). If you take part in a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us the name and email address you were invited with, as well as feedback, screenshots and crash reports that you choose to send. We use this only to run the test and improve the app, and we delete it when the test has ended, at the latest 12 months after it ends.

10. Our website (dayleaf.info)

Our website dayleaf.info is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, the page you open, the time and your browser type, in order to deliver the page and protect the service against misuse (server logs). We do not receive these logs and cannot see who visits the website.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; Article 5(2)(f) KVKK). Under the FADP, this processing is necessary to run the website.
  • Transfer to the USA: GitHub participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (including its UK extension) and the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For visitors from Turkey, the transfer takes place in accordance with Article 9 KVKK.
  • The website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no content loaded from other providers. Its fonts are stored on the website itself.

More information: GitHub's privacy statement, https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

11. Contacting us by email

If you write to us, we process the data in your message: your email address, your name if you give it, the content of your message and any attachments, and the time it was sent. We use this only to answer you and to deal with your request.

  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where your message relates to the use of the app, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering you, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Under the FADP, we process this data lawfully, for the purpose you can see, and only as far as necessary. Under KVKK, the processing is necessary to establish, exercise or protect a right, or for our legitimate interest without harming your fundamental rights (Article 5(2)(e) and (f) KVKK).
  • Retention: we delete your messages once your request has been fully dealt with, at the latest 12 months after our last exchange, unless the law requires us to keep them longer or they are needed to defend legal claims.
  • Service providers: our email is hosted by an email provider that processes messages on our behalf and under our instructions. If this provider stores data outside Switzerland, the EU/EEA or Turkey, we only use providers that offer the safeguards the law requires, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission's / Swiss standard contractual clauses.
  • Transfer abroad: we are based in Switzerland. If you write to us from the EU/EEA, your message is transferred to Switzerland, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection. If you write to us from Turkey, your message is transferred to Switzerland because you chose to contact us there. This transfer is occasional and necessary to answer you, in accordance with Article 9 KVKK.

We do not use your email for marketing, and we do not sell or pass on your data.

12. Children

Quoteleaf is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Children who want to write to us should ask a parent or guardian first. If we learn that we have received a message from a child under the age at which consent is required in their country, we will delete it once the request has been answered.

13. Security

The data described in section 4 is protected by your device's operating system: each app's storage is separated from other apps, and on current iPhones and most Android phones it is encrypted when the device is locked. Please protect your device with a passcode. Since we do not receive or store this data, it cannot be exposed through a data breach on our side.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights regarding personal data we hold about you (in practice, only email messages, section 11):

  • Under the Swiss FADP: the right to information (Art. 25), to rectification and deletion, to object to processing, to data portability (Art. 28), and to bring legal claims (Art. 32).
  • Under the GDPR (EU/EEA): the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
  • Under KVKK (Turkey), Article 11: to learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred, request correction, deletion or destruction and the notification of these to third parties, object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, write to support@dayleaf.info. This is free of charge. We answer within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity if this is needed to protect your data.

Data stored only on your device (section 4) is under your own control: you can view and change it in the app, and delete it by deleting the app.

15. Right to complain to a supervisory authority

You have the right to complain to a data protection authority:

  • Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC/EDÖB), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch
  • EU/EEA: the data protection authority of the country where you live or work, or where an alleged infringement took place.
  • Turkey: Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu), Ankara, www.kvkk.gov.tr. Under KVKK you first apply to us, the controller (Article 13 KVKK), and may then complain to the Board (Article 14 KVKK).

16. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app changes the way it handles data, for example if we add new features that require a connection to the internet or user accounts. Any such feature will only be added after this policy has been updated, and where the law requires it, only with your consent. The current version is always available in the app (Settings > Privacy policy) and at dayleaf.info/privacy. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

17. Language versions

This policy is available in English, German and Turkish. The English version is the original. Where the law of your country requires, the version in your language applies.
